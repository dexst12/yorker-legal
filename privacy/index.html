Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Overview

Yorker (“Yorker”, “we”, “our”) is an independent cricket scores, statistics, and news application. We believe in privacy by design: the app functions without tracking you, profiling you across the web, or selling your data.

1. Information Stored On Your Device

All personal preferences and profile details are stored locally on your device only:

  • Local Profile: If you use Login or Sign Up, your display name and email address are saved in local device storage. There are no remote user accounts or passwords transmitted to our servers in this version.
  • Preferences & Desk: Your pinned teams, favorite players, fantasy practice squad, poll selections, match reminder flags, and theme settings are saved locally via secure storage on your device.
  • Onboarding State: A simple boolean flag remembering you completed the intro tour.

2. Information Loaded From The Network

To provide real-time cricket updates, Yorker requests data from our third-party cricket data provider (PitchCore):

  • Live match scorecards, commentary feeds, series schedules, and points tables.
  • Official player statistics, world rankings, and editorial news articles.
  • Public team logos and player photos.

These requests send standard HTTP headers necessary to receive the data. We do not attach advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) or personal identifiers to these content requests.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not include third-party advertising tracking SDKs.
  • We do not process payments or store credit card details.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location.

4. Data Deletion & Your Rights

Because your profile and favorites reside solely on your device, you have complete and instantaneous control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to You → Delete local profile to permanently erase your profile name, email, favorites, reminders, and fantasy squad from your device.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the Yorker app from iOS or Android immediately deletes all application data stored in your local sandbox.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or Yorker’s data practices, please contact our support team:
