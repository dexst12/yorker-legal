Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Yorker (“Yorker”, “we”, “our”) is an independent cricket scores, statistics, and news application. We believe in privacy by design: the app functions without tracking you, profiling you across the web, or selling your data.

1. Information Stored On Your Device

Yorker does not require an account. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or a password, and it does not create a user profile.

  • Cache: Recently loaded match, series, news, and image data may stay on your device so screens open faster. That cache is cricket content, not information about you.
  • Match reminders: If you turn on a reminder, the app stores that match on your device and schedules a local notification. Reminders are not uploaded to Yorker. Turning the reminder off cancels that notification.
  • Uninstall: Removing Yorker deletes the cache and any reminders stored on the device.

2. Information Loaded From The Network

Yorker uses PitchCore for live scores, scorecards, commentary, fixtures, series, points tables, player statistics, rankings, and news. The app loads team logos and photos from the image hosts listed in the feed.

Versions that use Yorker’s server send cricket requests through its hosting provider, which receives your IP address and ordinary connection details such as request time. Yorker’s server briefly uses your IP address to limit abusive requests and sends cricket requests to PitchCore without including your IP address, advertising identifier, or user ID. Earlier versions connect to PitchCore directly, so PitchCore receives their ordinary connection details. Image hosts receive connection details when the app loads images.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not include third-party advertising tracking SDKs.
  • We do not process payments or store credit card details.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location.
  • Notification permission is used only for match reminders you turn on. Yorker does not send marketing pushes.

4. Children

Yorker is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
